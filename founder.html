A note from John

Why a hot dog brand?

Because familiar food can create a joyful, low-pressure place for people to be more curious about what they eat and who they meet.

The food is simple.
The people are not.

I have spent much of my life being difficult to categorize. I grew up on the north side of Minneapolis and attended private school on scholarship. Moving between those worlds taught me how much people share, and how quickly we place one another into boxes.

As a Black man, people have often carried a ready-made story about who I was supposed to be. I rarely fit it. My interests have never stayed in one lane either: business and technology, psychology and faith, art and interior design, civil and disability rights, fine wine and exceptional food. No single label explains me. I do not believe one label explains anyone else.

The first time I arrived in San Francisco, I fell in love with a city shaped by people who challenged old assumptions and built lives beyond accepted boxes. DogOnJohns is my way of participating in that tradition.

We make gourmet hot dogs because food gives people a familiar place to begin. The quality is serious: 100% organic, grass-fed beef franks; thoughtful preparation; house-made condiments; clear ingredients. The experience stays joyful.

Good food earns attention. Transparency builds trust. Then curiosity has room to enter. No one is told what to think, and no one is required to participate. We simply create an open table and let people take the conversation where it needs to go.

What we care about

Three values. Clearly lived.

01Food first.

Taste, texture and ingredient quality have to earn the attention before anything else can happen.

02Nothing hidden.

We say what we source, what we make and why each choice belongs on the dog.

03Stay curious.

We resist easy boxes and make room for light, generous conversation. Participation is always optional.

“I like what they’re about, and the dogs were so good. I have to tell someone.”

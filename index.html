Ingredient transparency

Nothing hidden behind “gourmet.”

The product proves the philosophy. We show what is sourced, what is made in-house and what still needs final operational validation.

01 / THE FRANKOrganic grass-fed beef.

The beef standard is named directly. Final public service materials will carry the received supplier label and allergen review.

02 / THE BUNToasted with grass-fed butter.

The brioche contains wheat, milk and egg. The toast is part of the texture standard, not decoration.

03 / THE HOUSERelishes and condiments made by us.

Mustards, dill relish, Verde relish and lime crema are house-made with short, recognizable recipes. Onion is fresh-cut for clean crunch.

04 / THE METHODPoached. Split. Seared.

The frank is gently poached, never boiled, to hold onto its juice and beef flavor. It is then split while attached and seared on both cut sides for browned edges, texture and snap.

Current truth boundary: these product images represent the approved builds. Before first public service, the site still needs the received supplier labels, final Signature Potato Crunch formulation, complete allergens and cross-contact disclosures.

Our brand

The hot dog is the invitation.

Food comes first. It has to taste exceptional before it can carry anything larger.

Then the table opens. Curious, complex people get room to laugh, listen and see one another more clearly.

Meet the founder

John built an open table.

John grew up on the North Side of Minneapolis, Minnesota, in a lower-income neighborhood. His interests cross business and technology; art and interior design; psychology and faith; civil and disability rights; fine wine and exceptional food. No single label explains him.

San Francisco showed John a city shaped by people who resisted old assumptions. DogOnJohns turns a familiar pleasure into common ground where complex people can meet without being flattened.
